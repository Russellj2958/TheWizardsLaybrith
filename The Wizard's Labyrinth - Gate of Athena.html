⚔️ The Wizard's Labyrinth ⚔️

A Journey Through Death and Mystery

📜 How to Play

Goal: Be the first traveler to reach Step 10 and claim the Heart of the Sun!

🎮 Game Rules

  • Choose Wisely: Each scenario has 3 choices with different outcomes:
    • ✅ Right Path = Move forward +1 step (play again immediately!)
    • ⬅️ Backwards = Move back -1 step (turn ends)
    • 💀 Death = Reset to Step 0 and rename your character
  • Multiplayer: Add up to 6 players - take turns making choices
  • The Graveyard: All deaths are remembered eternally
  • 5 Tiers: Scenarios get darker and stranger as you progress

🎵 Audio Features

  • Music: Upload your own atmospheric music files (bottom-right panel)
  • Volume Control: Adjust music volume with the slider
  • Voice Reading: Enable AI narrator to read scenarios aloud
  • Voice Control: Speak your choices (say "one", "two", or "three")

🎭 Choose Your Class

  • Spellblade: Master of arcane combat
  • Alchemist: Wielder of potions and transmutation
  • Paladin: Holy warrior of divine light
  • Shadow Weaver: Manipulator of darkness and secrets
